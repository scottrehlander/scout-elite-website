## Example format: Exeter Invitational (Dec 12-14, 2025)

Eight teams (Exeter, Brewster, Greenwich CDS, Kimball Union, Vermont, Gunn, North Yarmouth, Hill), three
rounds, every team plays three games over the weekend; no final or champion is listed. Results e.g.
Exeter 4 Brewster 2, Kimball Union 10 Greenwich CDS 0, Exeter 1 Hill 1 (OT). So holiday "tournaments" are
showcase weekends of three games, not knockout brackets (at least this one; check others before
generalizing).
