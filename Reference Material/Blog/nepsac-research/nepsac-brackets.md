## 1. How Large vs Small works, and how the 8 teams are picked (boys)

**Classification basis: boys' enrollment, grades 9-12, split evenly. VERIFIED** (By-Laws Art. VII.A.e):
> "NEPSBIHA schools will be divided evenly based upon male enrollment in grades 9-12 prior to the start of the season by the NEPSAC Director of Classification to determine if schools are considered "large" or "small" schools for the purpose of NEPSAC Tournament participation."

So there is no fixed enrollment threshold: it is a median split, redone before every season. NEPSAC publishes the resulting **lists**, not the enrollment numbers or the cut line.

**2026-27 boys lists. VERIFIED** (classification PDF, header "NEPSAC Classifications (LAST UPDATED 11/3/2026) Boys Ice Hockey"): 29 Large, 28 Small.
- Large: Andover, Exeter, Brunswick, Choate, Avon, Loomis, Milton, Belmont Hill, Deerfield, NMH, Salisbury, Hotchkiss, Taft, St. Sebastian's, BB&N, Thayer, Kent, Tabor, St. Paul's, Noble and Greenough, Trinity Pawling, Worcester, Dexter, Williston, Austin Prep, Westminster, Middlesex, Lawrence, Proctor.
- Small: Berkshire, Cushing, Governor's, Roxbury Latin, Brewster, Rivers, Frederick Gunn, St. George's, St. Mark's, Albany Academy, Brooks, Groton, Kimball Union, Pomfret, Canterbury, New Hampton, Holderness, Millbrook, Pingree, Hoosac, Portsmouth Abbey, WMA, Winchendon, Vermont, Berwick, Tilton, Hebron, Kents Hill.

**How often reclassified: every season (rule above: "prior to the start of the season"). VERIFIED**, and the lists do move. Example: Cushing won the 2026 *Large* (Martin/Earl) bracket (2026 bracket PDF, Martin/Earl page: "2 Cushing Academy ... Champions") but is on the 2026-27 *Small* list. Lawrence Academy won the Small bracket in 2018 and 2023 and is on the 2026-27 Large list. (Inference from two VERIFIED documents.) Parent takeaway: a school's bracket can change from one year to the next.

**The three tournaments. VERIFIED** (Art. VII.A.c):
> "There will be three NEPSAC Tournaments upon completion of the regular season. Each tournament will consist of 8 teams. The Stuart/Corkery Tournament (this will comprise of the top 8 schools in NEPSBIHA); the Martin/Earl Tournament (this will comprise of the top 8 large schools remaining after the Stuart/Corkery Tournament has been assigned); the Piatelli/Simmons Tournament (this will comprise of the top 8 small schools remaining after the Stuart/Corkery Tournament has been assigned)."

**Selection math: RPI, plus JSPR for the Elite 8. VERIFIED** (Art. VII.A.d-e):
> "Placement in the three NEPSAC Tournaments will be determined by the calculations of the RPI (explained below) and JSPR (explained below)."
- Elite 8: "the top 16 teams based on the RPI are entered into the JSPR system ... based on four criteria. 1. Head to head result. 2. Comparing the teams RPI. 3. Record against mutual opponents. 4. Record against teams under consideration". Each pairwise comparison awards a "JSPR point"; "The top 8 teams after JSPA points have been awarded will be ranked based on their final point total."
- Large and Small brackets: by RPI alone: "Teams will be determined by the RPI."
- RPI weights: own winning % "25%", opponents' winning % "21%", opponents' opponents' winning % "54%". Ties count half ("wins plus ½ the number of ties"). Plain-language point: more than half of a team's RPI is strength of schedule, which is why a schedule full of strong opponents matters.
- Regular-season shootouts count as ties: "a shootout may occur but the game will be recorded as a tie for NEPSBIHA tournament selection ranking purposes."

**Eligibility minimums. VERIFIED**:
- "Schools must play a minimum of fifteen NEPSBIHA contests during the regular season in order to be eligible for tournament participation."
- Cap: "Only 35 contests will be allowed during the course of the year. This includes scrimmages." Exceed it and "they will be deemed ineligible for tournament consideration that year."
- Only games against NEPSBIHA teams and Associate Members count; "Associate Members are not eligible for NEPSAC Tournament participation."
- Admin: dues "by October 15th or schools will not be eligible"; missing "more than two meetings in a row" makes a school ineligible.

**Who decides, and the seed tweak. VERIFIED**: a Tournament Selection Committee chaired by the coaches' association president, with officers and league reps (Art. IV). It "will meet on the final Sunday of the season after all regular season games have been completed." It "reserves the right to move seeds one place higher or lower while ensuring that home ice is guaranteed to the top 4 seeds in each tournament" (to avoid neutral-site games). "Host school will be the team with higher seed." A neutral site is used if the drive is over "2:45" by Google Maps.

**Schedule. VERIFIED**: "The quarterfinals and semi-finals will be played on the Wednesday and Saturday following the final regular season. The Championship games will be played at a predetermined site on the Sunday following the semi-finals." Sudden-death OT in playoffs is "18-minute" periods.

**When the format began. SECONDARY (NEPSAC news article)**: https://nepsac.org/nepsac-girls-ice-hockey-association-honors-coaches-with-tournaments-names-2 (Bob York, 3/2/2020) quotes the girls' co-president: "... similar to the boys tournament, which took on this format back in the 2008-2009 season." The archives have three-bracket PDFs from 2010 on (the 2009 PDF would not text-extract; I did not confirm it).

In 2026 the regular season ended Sat. Feb. 28 and quarterfinals were Wed. March 4 (2026 bracket PDF: "Wednesday, March 4 ... Saturday, March 7 ... Sunday, March 8").
- **2027 dates and venues: UNKNOWN.** NEPSAC's tournament page lists only the fall 2026 championships so far (https://nepsac.org/tournaments/tournament-postseason/: "2026 Fall Championships"). Inference only: boys' finals have fallen on the first or second Sunday of March (Mar 2 2014, Mar 3 2019/2024, Mar 5 2023, Mar 6 2022, Mar 8 2020/2026, Mar 9 2025), so **Sunday, March 7, 2027** (quarterfinals Wed. ## 3. The 2026-27 season

- **Start date: UNKNOWN (no official NEPSAC date).** The by-laws set no opening day. They only freeze schedules once play starts: "After the completion of that schools first regular season game no additional games may be added/scheduled except as a scrimmage." SECONDARY: U.S. Hockey Report, https://www.ushr.com/news/neprep/20261101 (Sun. 11/1/26): "The 2026-27 Prep Pages are now officially open ... as coaches begin to upload their teams' schedules." Prep hockey has traditionally opened after Thanksgiving, but I did not find a source for that.
- **Classification for 2026-27 is out** (boys updated 11/3/2026, girls 10/16/2026; VERIFIED, above). Boys' Large/Small membership changed from last year (Cushing now Small, per the 2026 Large bracket win vs. the new list).
- **By-laws revised 2-25-26** (the filename and URL date say so; VERIFIED). The document's header still reads "Adopted 10/26/2017", and I found no changelog. **UNKNOWN** which clauses changed.
- **Equipment rules for 2026-27. VERIFIED**, https://assets-rst7.rschooltoday.com/rst7files/uploads/sites/328/2026/10/22085954/NEPSAC-Executive-Board-Hockey-Equipment-Reminders-for-the-2026-2027-Season.pdf: neck guards, internal mouthguards, earpieces and J-clips are all required ("NEPSAC requires all NEPSAC hockey players to wear a properly fitting hockey-approved neck guard"). This repeats existing rules: neck guards have been mandatory since "the 2024-2025 season for all games and practices", after a player's death from a skate laceration in 2021-22. Nothing new this year.
- **New members or league realignment for 2026-27: UNKNOWN.** None found in NEPSAC documents or searches. Boys' association officers for 2026-27 are listed (President Scott Harff, St. Paul's; VERIFIED, NEPSBIHA page).
- Girls' association 2026-27 presidents: Christa Talbot Syfu (Williston) and Claudia Asano Barcomb (Governor's) (VERIFIED, NEPSGIHA page).
