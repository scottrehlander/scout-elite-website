Research date: 2026-11-04 (start of the 2026-27 season). Every quote below is from a page or PDF opened during
this research. Downloaded PDFs are in

Wikipedia. **UNKNOWN** = no published rule found. **INFERENCE** = drawn from rosters or school listings, not a rule.

## Girls vs boys

- No NEPSAC or NEPSGIHA rule treats girls' PGs differently: the girls' constitution (2025-26) has no
  player-eligibility section at all, so the NEPSAC age rule is the only individual limit. VERIFIED (absence).
- The ISL ban covers all ISL teams, girls included (the Essential Understandings are league-wide). VERIFIED.
- PGs are rarer in girls' prep hockey. SECONDARY: Hockey Journal girls coaches survey, July 21, 2025: "Some players
  may play postgraduate seasons." One coach: "with the five-year eligibility model, it's dependent on how many of the
  kids are not already reclassified ... That ultimately limits the number of kids that are eligible to PG." It also
  names a player who will "play a postgrad season at Williston Northampton this winter."
  https://hockeyjournal.com/what-does-the-future-of-girls-prep-hockey-look-like-girls-prep-coaches-survey-part-3/

## Related context for parents (not a conference rule)

- NCAA age-based "five-in-five" eligibility, effective for prospects who first enroll full time in fall 2027 or
  later. SECONDARY (a prep-school explainer, Aug 6, 2026): "does a gap year or postgraduate year start the clock?
  No — not by itself. A gap or postgraduate year only starts the clock if the student competes for a college team
  during it, or turns nineteen during it." https://insights.newtonprep.org/ncaa-adopts-age-based-eligibility-rules/
  The "turns nineteen during it" wording reads as looser than the same article's own Sept 1 trigger description;
  check against NCAA's own text before stating it in the post.

## Gaps and access notes

  TNPHL, MAPHL or the USHR "Mid Atlantic" group. The answer for those is "NEPSAC rules apply; no league PG rule
  published." Confirming means asking a league athletic director.
