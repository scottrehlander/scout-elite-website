Bar Down Prototype

This one is a prototype. Rough edges, missing polish, and half-formed ideas are all expected. If you play it, tell us what worked and what did not: share feedback.

You are looking straight down at two nets lying mouth-up. Tap to keep the puck in the air; it never stops moving sideways and it ricochets off the boards, so pick a net and let it drop in. Straight down the middle is a bar down: double points, and it is the only thing that raises your multiplier. Miss both nets and the multiplier is gone. The clock is the only thing that can end you, and every goal buys more of it.

Bar Down

Tap to lift the puck. Drop it into a net before the clock runs out, and aim for the middle: that is a bar down, and it builds your multiplier.

Phone: tap anywhere on the ice. Keyboard: space or the up arrow. Dead centre of a net scores double and builds the multiplier.
