Bar Down Prototype

This one is a prototype. Rough edges, missing polish, and half-formed ideas are all expected. If you play it, tell us what worked and what did not: share feedback.

Tap to keep the puck in the air. It never stops moving sideways and it ricochets off the boards, so pick your moment and drop it in under the bar. The clock is the only thing that can end you, and every goal buys more of it. Let the puck hit the ice and your streak dies, so the greedy run is the high score.

Bar Down

Tap to lift the puck. Put it in under the crossbar before the clock runs out.

Phone: tap anywhere on the ice. Keyboard: space or the up arrow. Top of the net scores double.
