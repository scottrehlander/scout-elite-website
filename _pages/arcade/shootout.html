Shootout Prototype

Penalty shots against a goalie who keeps a book on you. Wait for the release meter, then tap the spot you want: high glove, high blocker, low corners, or the five-hole. Hit the green window or he reads it. Mix up your spots, he remembers them between visits.

Shootout

Time the green window, tap your spot. Three saves ends the run.

Phone: tap a spot in the net while the meter is live. Keyboard: 1 glove high, 2 blocker high, 3 glove low, 4 blocker low, 5 five-hole.
