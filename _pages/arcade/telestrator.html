The Telestrator Prototype

This one is a prototype. Rough edges, missing polish, and half-formed ideas are all expected. If you play it, tell us what worked and what did not: share feedback.

Every coach has drawn the perfect route on the tablet. Here is the catch: your skater runs EXACTLY what you draw. Every wiggle, every loop, every shaky bit of finger art. Draw him a path through the defense, and when the line ends, he shoots for the far post. As you draw, the ice shows you the read: where the defenders will be when you get there, a red X if one of them gets a stick on you, and a shot line that turns green when you have a lane. Ink is limited, so keep it clean.

The Telestrator

Drag a route from your skater. He runs it exactly as drawn, then shoots at the far post. Watch the ghosts as you draw: they are where the defense ends up, and a green shot line means you have a lane.

Phone: draw the route with your finger, then use the buttons. Lift and touch again to add to the same route. Desktop: click and drag also works. Enter runs the play, R redraws.
