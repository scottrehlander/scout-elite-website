Zamboni Prototype

The sheet is chewed up and the next shift is waiting. Pick a direction and the Zamboni glides until it hits the boards, resurfacing every tile it crosses. Clean all the scuffed ice in as few passes as you can. Beat par for three stars.

Zamboni

Swipe on the ice and the Zamboni slides until it hits something. No brakes out there.
